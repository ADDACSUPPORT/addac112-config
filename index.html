Edit your 112's banks from a computer

  1. Turn the 112 off and put its SD card in your computer.
  2. Open SD card… and choose the card (or one bank folder).
  3. Open SD card folder… and choose the card (or one bank folder).
  4. Edit the bank settings, presets, scales or the global settings, then Save.
  5. Put the card back in the 112 and load the bank.

Edit with the card out of the module: the 112 rewrites its files every time the bank is saved, so changes made on the module after opening the card here would be overwritten (and vice versa).

This browser can't write to the card. Saving downloads the edited files, and the page tells you where to copy each one. Chrome and Edge can open and save the card directly.

Everything happens in your browser: no files are uploaded anywhere.
