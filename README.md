# ADDAC112 config panel

**Open the panel: https://addacsupport.github.io/addac112-config/**

Edit your ADDAC112's SD card from a computer, without going through the module's menus:

- **Bank settings:** the settings a bank uses when no preset is selected.
- **Presets:** knob and switch values, options and loop list. You can also create, duplicate and delete presets.
- **Scales:** the Default and Custom sets of 7 scales. Ratios can be decimals or fractions such as `9/8`.
- **Global:** the bank loaded at start and the output gains.

## How to use it

1. Turn the 112 off and put its SD card in your computer.
2. Open the panel in **Chrome** or **Edge**, click **Open SD card…** and choose the card.
3. Edit, then click **Save**. The first time a file is saved, the original is kept next to it as a `.BAK` file.
4. Put the card back in the 112.

In other browsers (Firefox, Safari), the panel opens the card folder for reading, and **Download** gives you the changed files to copy onto the card.

Keep the card out of the module while you edit, because the 112 rewrites these files whenever it saves a bank.

Everything runs in your browser. No files are uploaded anywhere.
